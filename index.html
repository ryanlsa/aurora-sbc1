    Model and limitations

    Search uses randomized simulated annealing with restarts and validates each reported result against the selected constraints. It is not an exhaustive proof of minimum cost. Rating follows the FIFA 21 corrected rating formula. Chemistry uses a transparent approximation of legacy chemistry: same league, nation and club contribute one link point each; natural position chemistry is 3 + 6 × link ratio up to ratio 1, then increases to 10 at ratio 1.6, rounded down. Related positions reduce chemistry by 1 before optional loyalty; individual cap 10 and team cap 100. Formation links are editable estimates, not independently verified against Aurora. No manager or position modifier costs. Duplicate player detection uses normalized name + nation (conservative because the CSV has no asset IDs). Always verify in Aurora's squad builder before buying.
