    Model and limitations

    Search uses randomized simulated annealing with restarts and validates each reported result against the selected constraints. It is not an exhaustive proof of minimum cost. Rating follows the FIFA 21 corrected rating formula. Chemistry uses FIFA 21 link-strength bands, position compatibility, Icon and Hero links, and optional loyalty. Individual chemistry is capped at 10 and team chemistry at 100. Manager bonuses are excluded. Enter modifier prices to include them in spending. The bundled database identifies players by asset ID; legacy imports without a known ID fall back to name and nationality. Always verify in Aurora's squad builder before buying.
